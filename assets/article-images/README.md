## Hermes image policy

1. Prefer approved real product and company photography.
2. Use the canonical T500 reference whenever the T500 appears.
3. Generate only supporting composition, environment, abstract security/evidence motifs, and explanatory illustrations.
4. Never fabricate product specifications, product interfaces, evidence screens, agency insignia, customers, or operational scenes presented as documentary fact.
5. Every image plan must include its role, placement, source, aspect ratio, alt text, and caption.
